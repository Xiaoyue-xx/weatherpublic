    Recommendation method

    Coded, consistent guidance

    The app uses the typical apparent temperature from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. to choose a base outfit, then applies separate rain, snow, wind, UV, heat, temperature-swing, and thunderstorm rules.

    Suggestions are practical guidance for planning a day outside. They are not medical advice, emergency alerts, or a guarantee of comfort.

    Data and guidance

    Sources

    • Open-Meteo Forecast API provides weather and geocoding data under CC BY 4.0.
    • National Weather Service cold-weather guidance informs layering and weather-resistant clothing.
    • CDC extreme-heat guidance informs lightweight clothing and hydration reminders.
    • EPA UV Index guidance informs sun-protection reminders.

    Privacy

    Your latest location stays on this device

    The app sends a searched city or device coordinates to Open-Meteo only to retrieve weather. Device location is requested only after you choose “Use my current location.”

    Local storage contains one latest location with coordinates rounded to three decimal places, plus units, character, skin tone, and style. There is no account, analytics identifier, favorites list, location history, or stored weather history.

    Accessibility

    Designed for flexible access

    The interface supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader status announcements, text alternatives for meaningful artwork, large touch targets, reduced motion, and responsive text and layouts.
